## Description

AMD announced the RDNA 3 Radeon RX 7900 XTX on 3 November 2022, available from 13 December 2022, and calls
the RX 7900 series the world's first gaming graphics cards with a chiplet design. It combines a 5nm
Graphics Compute Die with six 6nm Memory Cache Dies and adds new AI instructions.
