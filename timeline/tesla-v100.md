## Description

NVIDIA announced the Tesla V100, its first Volta-based processor, on 10 May 2017. It has 640 Tensor Cores
rated at 120 teraflops of deep learning performance, 900 GB/s HBM2 memory, and next-generation NVLink with
up to 2x the throughput of the prior generation.
