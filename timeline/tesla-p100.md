## Description

NVIDIA announced the Pascal-based Tesla P100 at GTC on 5 April 2016. It combines NVLink, with 160 GB/s
of bidirectional interconnect bandwidth, and 16 GB of CoWoS HBM2 stacked memory at 720 GB/s.
