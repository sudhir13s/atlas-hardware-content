## Description
The GTX 680 introduced NVIDIA's Kepler architecture, shifting from Fermi's high-clock design to many
efficient CUDA cores under the SMX multiprocessor. Kepler-class Tesla cards (K40, K80) went on to power
the first wave of deep-learning research.
