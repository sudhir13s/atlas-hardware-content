## Description
NVIDIA launched the GeForce GTX 680 on 22 March 2012 as one of its first Kepler GeForce GPUs, built on
28 nm. Kepler's SMX multiprocessor runs at the graphics clock instead of Fermi's doubled shader clock;
the GTX 680 has eight SMX units and 1,536 CUDA cores.
