## Description

NVIDIA announced the H100, its first Hopper GPU, at GTC on 22 March 2022, with availability from the third
quarter. It combines fourth-generation Tensor Cores, the FP8 Transformer Engine, HBM3 memory (NVIDIA states
it is the first GPU with HBM3) and fourth-generation NVLink with 900 GB/s total bandwidth.
