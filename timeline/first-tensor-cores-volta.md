## Description
Volta, announced on 10 May 2017, introduced the Tensor Core: a unit that multiplies FP16 matrices and
accumulates in FP16 or FP32. NVIDIA's whitepaper states that V100's new Tensor Cores deliver up to 12x
higher peak TFLOPS for training and 6x for inference than the Pascal-based P100.
