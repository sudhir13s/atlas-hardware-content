## Description
NVIDIA announced the GeForce RTX 30 Series on 1 September 2020; the RTX 3090 became available on
24 September 2020 with 24 GB of GDDR6X. NVIDIA describes that memory as able to handle demanding AI
algorithms and large content-creation workloads.
