## Description

NVIDIA launched the Turing architecture at SIGGRAPH on 13 August 2018 with RT Cores, dedicated units that
accelerate ray tracing, alongside Tensor Cores. NVIDIA states that Turing enables the world's first
ray-tracing GPU. Its whitepaper introduces Deep Learning Super Sampling (DLSS), a Tensor Core-powered
technique that builds the final image from fewer input samples.
