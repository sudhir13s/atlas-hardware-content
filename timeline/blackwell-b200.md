## Description
NVIDIA announced Blackwell at GTC on 18 March 2024. A Blackwell GPU joins two reticle-limit dies over a
10 TB/s chip-to-chip link into one unified GPU, and its second-generation Transformer Engine adds 4-bit
floating point (FP4) inference. The GB200 superchip connects two B200 GPUs to a Grace CPU; GB200 NVL72
combines 36 of them (72 GPUs) over fifth-generation NVLink.
