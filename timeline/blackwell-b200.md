## Description
Announced at GTC 2024, Blackwell's B200 is a dual-die GPU presented as one accelerator, with FP4/FP6
microscaling and a second-generation Transformer Engine. The GB200 superchip pairs two B200s with a
Grace CPU for rack-scale NVLink domains (GB200 NVL72).
