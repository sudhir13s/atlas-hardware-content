## Description
NVIDIA announced the GeForce RTX 40 Series on 20 September 2022; the RTX 4090 became available on
12 October 2022. Ada Lovelace uses a custom TSMC 4N process, fourth-generation Tensor Cores with FP8,
third-generation RT Cores and DLSS 3 frame generation; the RTX 4090 has 24 GB of GDDR6X.
