## Description

NVIDIA announced on 14 May 2020 that the A100, the first GPU based on the Ampere architecture, was in
full production and shipping. Its third-generation Tensor Cores added TF32 and BF16 operations, and it
introduced fine-grained structured sparsity and Multi-Instance GPU (MIG), which splits one A100 into up
to seven instances.
