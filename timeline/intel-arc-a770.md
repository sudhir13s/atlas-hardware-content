## Description

The Intel Arc A770 went on sale on 12 October 2022. It uses the Xe-HPG microarchitecture with 32 Xe-cores
and 512 Intel Xe Matrix Extensions (XMX) engines, and Intel lists ray tracing, Xe Super Sampling (XeSS)
upscaling and AV1 video encoding across the Arc A-series.
