## Description
The Xe-HPG-based Arc A770 was Intel's first competitive discrete gaming GPU, shipping with XMX AI matrix
engines, hardware ray tracing, XeSS upscaling, and full AV1 encode — establishing Intel as a third GPU
vendor.
