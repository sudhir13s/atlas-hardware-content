## Description
NVIDIA launched the Turing-based Tesla T4 at GTC Japan on 12 September 2018 as part of an inference
platform. It is a 75-watt PCIe card with 320 Turing Tensor Cores and multi-precision support from FP32
down to INT4, rated at 130 TOPS for INT8 and 260 TOPS for INT4.
