## Description

NVIDIA announced the GeForce RTX 50 Series at CES on 6 January 2025; the RTX 5090 became available on
30 January 2025. It has fifth-generation Tensor Cores, 32 GB of GDDR7 and DLSS 4, and NVIDIA states the
RTX 50 Series are the first consumer GPUs to support FP4 precision.
