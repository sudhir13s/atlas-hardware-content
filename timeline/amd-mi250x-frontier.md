## Description

On 30 May 2022 AMD announced that Oak Ridge's Frontier supercomputer, built with AMD EPYC processors and
Instinct MI250X accelerators, measured 1.1 exaflops and was, in AMD's words, the first to break the
exascale barrier. AMD had announced the CDNA 2 MI200 series on 8 November 2021 as its first multi-die GPU.
