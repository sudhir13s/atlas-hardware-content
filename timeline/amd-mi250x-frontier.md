## Description
The dual-die CDNA 2 MI250X, with full-rate FP64 matrix math, powers Oak Ridge's Frontier — the first
supercomputer to break the exascale (10^18 FLOPS) barrier in 2022 — establishing AMD as a top-tier HPC
accelerator vendor.
