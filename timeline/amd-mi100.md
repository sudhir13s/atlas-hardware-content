## Description
The first CDNA accelerator, the MI100, added AMD's Matrix Cores (MFMA instructions) — its answer to
Tensor Cores — and dropped the graphics pipeline entirely, marking AMD's split into separate gaming
(RDNA) and datacenter (CDNA) architectures.
