## Description

AMD announced the availability of the CDNA 3 Instinct MI300X on 6 December 2023, with 192 GB of HBM3 and
5.3 TB/s peak memory bandwidth, aimed at large language model (LLM) training and inference. The MI300A
accelerated processing unit (APU), announced alongside it, pairs CDNA 3 with "Zen 4" CPU cores.
