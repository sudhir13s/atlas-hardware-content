## Definition

BF16 (bfloat16) is a 16-bit floating-point format with one sign bit, eight exponent bits and seven
mantissa bits. Google states that its dynamic range equals that of 32-bit FP32 while it uses half the
memory, and that it has more exponent bits than IEEE 16-bit FP16.

## Why You Care

Google states that FP16 training typically needs loss scaling, while BF16 comes close to being a drop-in
replacement for FP32. Whether a chip lists BF16 (not only FP16) tells you whether it supports that
16-bit training path.
