## Definition
BF16 (brain floating point) is a 16-bit format with 8 exponent bits and 7 mantissa bits — the same
dynamic range as 32-bit FP32 but far less precision. It differs from IEEE FP16, which spends more bits on
mantissa and less on exponent (5 exponent, 10 mantissa).

## Why You Care
BF16's wide exponent range makes it much more forgiving for training than FP16: it rarely overflows or
underflows, so models often train in BF16 with little or no loss scaling. That robustness made BF16 the
default training precision on modern accelerators. Knowing whether a chip supports BF16 (not just FP16)
tells you whether it can train large models comfortably at 16-bit.
