## Overview
Radeon RX cards across the RDNA generations:

- **RX 5700 XT** (RDNA).
- **RX 6900 XT** (RDNA 2), with AMD Infinity Cache and hardware ray tracing acceleration.
- **RX 7900 XTX** (RDNA 3), with AI Accelerators. AMD announced it with the RX 7900 XT as the world's
  first gaming graphics cards with an AMD chiplet design.
