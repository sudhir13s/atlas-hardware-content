## Overview
The consumer Radeon RX line across the RDNA generations: from the gaming-focused RDNA 1 (RX 5700 XT), to
Infinity Cache and hardware ray tracing in RDNA 2 (RX 6900 XT), to the first chiplet consumer GPU with AI
accelerators in RDNA 3 (RX 7900 XTX).
