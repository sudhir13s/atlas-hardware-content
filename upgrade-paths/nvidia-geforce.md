## Overview

A high-end GeForce card from each series, by announcement year. The RTX 2080 Ti (Turing) is the first
card on this path with **RT Cores** and **Tensor Cores**. Memory went from **11 GB** (GTX 1080 Ti, RTX 2080 Ti) to **24 GB**
(RTX 3090, RTX 4090) and **32 GB** (RTX 5090). NVIDIA names **FP8** for the RTX 40 Series Tensor Cores and
**FP4** for the RTX 50 Series.
